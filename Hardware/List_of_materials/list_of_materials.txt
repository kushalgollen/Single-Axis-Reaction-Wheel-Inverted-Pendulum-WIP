Materials Lists & required parts:

* Simple FOC Shield with integrated esp32 or u can get these parts also separately

* BLDC Gimbal Motor: GM4108H-120T without slip ring and 'H' hollow shaft

* Custom Flywheel: using 3d-printed body mounted with screws

* AS5600 Magnetic Encoder (I2C)

* MPU6050 IMU Gyroscope & Accelerometer (I2C)

* USB-UART Module FTDI232RL or CP2102: to connect esp32 to computer

* LiPo Battery 4s or 6s: I'll be using my DIY benchtop power supply

* Screws: M2.5 or M3, max 3-4mm thread

* Electric wires: silicone, low-resistance and low-noise signal distribution
